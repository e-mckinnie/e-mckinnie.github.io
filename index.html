Hi, I’m Elizabeth McKinnie

PhD student in Information Science at CU Boulder

I study recommender systems -- the algorithmic systems that are responsible for recommending content on your TikTok feed, ordering news, or suggesting your next Amazon purchase. I'm particularly passionate about transparency and control, allowing users of recommender systems to understand how the algorithm works and having input into it. I strive for a mixed methods approach to my research, and am currently interested in co-design and participatory AI methods.

I hold a bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Applied and Computational Mathematical Sciences (ACMS) from the University of Washington. Prior to grad school, I worked as a software engineer at Microsoft.

Outside of research, I love to bicycle, hike, and crochet.
